# Week 1 assignment
Work individually on the same dataset before collaborating. Use the starter notebook as a scaffold, not a finished solution.

- Load the dataset and report shape, types, missing values, duplicated track IDs and numerical ranges.
- Decide how to handle missing values and repeated tracks; explain the effect of your choices on genre information.
- Produce at least four labelled plots: feature distribution, energy vs valence scatter, feature correlation heatmap and a genre comparison of your choice.
- Report three observations supported by data, including one unexpected result.
- Propose a mood mapping using valence and energy; inspect several tracks and discuss counterexamples. Do not claim the mapping is ground truth.
- Explain why tempo, loudness and bounded features may need scaling before distance-based recommendation.
- Propose one experiment or improvement to investigate in Weeks 2–3, with an evaluation plan.
- Submit a reproducible notebook and completed submission template. Include the source link and cleaning rationale. Do not upload the raw dataset.

No need to train a model this week. Evaluate the quality of reasoning, reproducibility and evidence rather than the number of charts.
