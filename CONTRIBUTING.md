# Moodify collaboration
Use a fork or personal branch; do not push directly to main. Keep commits focused, document experiments and request mentor review. Put notebooks in a personal submissions folder. Clear unnecessary outputs before committing. Do not commit raw datasets, credentials, virtual environments or personal information. Link to the original dataset and review its license before redistribution or public deployment. Record preprocessing choices, random seeds where applicable, limitations and reproducible steps. Discuss overlapping ideas with teammates. When blocked, share what you tried, the error and a minimal reproducible example.
